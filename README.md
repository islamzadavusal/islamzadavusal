## 👋 About me

I'm a **software developer based in Baku** who builds native **iOS & Android** apps, websites, backends and AI-powered products. Over **4+ years** I've shipped **30+ apps** across **10+ companies** — from a banking app used by millions to AR/LiDAR scanners and generative AI tools.

I work at **Kapital Bank**, on **Birbank** and **Birbank Business**, Azerbaijan's leading digital banking apps. Outside work I design, build and publish my own products end to end: games, calculators, AI tools and developer utilities.

> **One place, one person accountable.** Mobile, web and backend — clean architecture, secure data flows and polished UX.
